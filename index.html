How it works

Tears are 1TEAR tokens. You offer them by sending them to the Vault, an account with no keys, so nobody can ever move them again.

This is not a burn: the token's total supply stays the same, but the tokens you offered are out of circulation for good. The transfer is public on NEAR. Your note is stored in this site's database, not on the blockchain, but it is tied to your transfer: the transfer carries a fingerprint (hash) of your note, and a note is pinned only after the server has checked the transfer on NEAR.

The Vault is 1tearvault.oxkraven.tg. Anyone can check it on NearBlocks: it has no access keys and no contract code, so no one can move what is sent to it.

1TEAR is a community project. Early version.
