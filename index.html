How it works

Tears are 1TEAR tokens. You offer them by sending them to the Vault, an account with no keys, so nobody can ever move them again.

This is not a burn: the token's total supply stays the same, but the tokens you offered are out of circulation for good. Your note is stored on this site. The transfer is public on NEAR, and every note links to it.

1TEAR is a community project. This preview is a design draft.
